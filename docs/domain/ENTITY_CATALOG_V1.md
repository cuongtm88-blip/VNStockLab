# VNStockLab Entity Catalog V1

**Status:** Frozen  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-16

## Catalog conventions

- Identities below are logical identifiers; no storage type or key-generation mechanism is prescribed.
- **Immutable** means the historical record is not edited after its stated publication or finalization point. Corrections and re-evaluations create new records with lineage.
- **Mutable definition** means controlled edits are allowed until execution or publication requires a frozen version or snapshot.
- **Shared** means platform-wide data. **User-owned** means exactly one user owns the record and access must be isolated by that user.
- All timestamps include an unambiguous timezone or market-time interpretation.

### Technical Score structured value

Technical Score is modeled within `SignalEvidence` as category contribution records, optionally assembled into a typed structured value. The frozen maximum contributions are:

| Category | Maximum points |
| --- | ---: |
| Trend | 25 |
| Momentum | 20 |
| Volume | 15 |
| Money Flow | 15 |
| Market Structure | 15 |
| Pattern Confirmation | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Every contribution references its deterministic supporting evidence and calculation/strategy version. The total is the sum of category contributions and cannot exceed 100. A high Technical Score is evidence only and does not automatically mean or create a Buy signal.
