# Product Vision

VNStockLab aims to be a comprehensive, transparent, and dependable workspace for technical analysis and investment decision support in the Vietnamese stock market. It will bring market exploration, deterministic analysis, strategy evaluation, portfolio oversight, and clear explanations into a unified web platform.

The product is intended to help users understand why a signal was produced, inspect the underlying evidence, compare scenarios, and make informed decisions without presenting uncertain analysis as fact.

## Major Capabilities

- Market dashboard
- Stock search
- Candlestick charts
- Technical indicators
- Ichimoku analysis
- Candlestick and price patterns
- Support and resistance analysis
- Dow Theory analysis
- Elliott Wave scenarios
- Stock scanner
- Signal scoring
- Backtesting
- Portfolio management
- Risk management
- Sector rotation analysis
- Alerts
- Trading journal
- AI-generated explanations

## Role of AI

AI-generated explanations will translate deterministic analysis results into clear, accessible language and provide context for users. AI must not fabricate market data, indicator values, signals, patterns, or other technical-analysis results. All quantitative values and signals must come from traceable, deterministic calculations performed by the platform.
